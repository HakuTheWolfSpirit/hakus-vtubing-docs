# Making an Avatar from Marketplace Components.

With this approach you get the ease of making a premade avatar like in VRoid or sims combined with the limits that Unity offers. Generally this is targeted as VRChat avatars, but fear not. They can be ported into all applications and even into .VRM.

The costs of this will be around 100$. This is neither the floor nor the cap. This is just what you should have available to play around with.

In general the process is picking an avatar base you like, picking some hair, picking an outfit and editing the textures before you export the avatar. Doing 3D modelling is possible but not required (for some parts like the avatar base I actually advise against editing it).

## Which Marketplace to Choose?

There are generally only two options viable right now: Jinxxy and BOOTH. Which one you pick is simple. If you want a human avatar pick BOOTH. If you want an animal/Furry avatar pick Jinxxy. I don't have experience with furry avatars myself, but please feel free to write an article about this yourself. The remaining documentation will be targeted specifically for human avatars. But I can imagine that the things you do are just the same with a few tools not being available to use.

### Chibi Avatars

For chibi avatars there are two options viable. They are viable because they have already a ton of clothes and add-ons available that natively fit for their model. 

These avatars are great because they have thousands of items and add-ons to chose from.

- [Kipfel](https://boothplorer.com/avatar/5813187) 
- [Mamehimita](https://boothplorer.com/avatar/4340548)

Often people pick both a female and a chibi avatar. Chibi avatars are simplified humans and their movement will also look simplified. 


### Male Avatars

There are a few Male bases available, but the available outfits are very limited. You might need to retexture some items to make your model unique. If you don't care about that and just want to assemble something that works you should still keep in mind that none of the male characters have >1k items to chose from. 

### Female Avatars

Female avatars are what the majority of players uses. There a a few female bases out with more than thousand items available and some are also able to wear outfits from some other models :\) 

I recommend anyone who is into having a lot of different outfits to choose either female or one of the two recommended chibi avatars. 

You can either choose a base which has many outfits or you can choose a base that has a mochi fitter profile available.
