# Making an Avatar from Marketplace Components.

With this approach you get the ease of making a premade avatar like in vroid or sims combined with the limits that unity offers. Generally this is targeted as VRChat avatars, but fear not. They can be ported into all applications and even into .VRM.

The costs of this will be around 100$. This is neither the lower nor upper ceiling. This is just what you should have available to play around with.

In general the process is picking an avatar base you like, picking some hair, picking an outfit and editing the textures before you export the avatar. Doing 3D modelling is possible but not required (for some parts like the avatar base I actually advice against editing it).

## Which market place to choose?

There are generally only two options viable right now: Jinxxy and BOOTH. Which one you pick is simple. If you want a human avatar pick BOOTH. If you want an animal/Furry avatar pick Jinxxy. I don't have experience with furry avatars myself, but please feel free to write an article about this yourself. The remaining documentation will be targeted specifically for human avatars. But I can imagine that the things you do are just the same with a few tools not being available to use.

## Choosing your Avatar Base

> Woah wait a minute, are you not gonna explain things first? 

No! Because there is not much you need to know beside that I describe in this topic. For choosing the avatar I recommend https://boothplorer.com/avatars for english natives. Japanese natives might be able to find their way round https://booth.pm but boothplorer also offers the ability to find clothes that fit your avatar base easily. Please keep in mind that boothplorer is a third party tool and scrapes data. Therefore you should always verify the information you see on the original page. Even if you translate the entire page from japanese to english.

### Male Avatars

There are a few Male bases available, but the available outfits are very limited. You might need to retexture some items to make your model unique. If you don't care about that and just want to assemble something that works you should still keep in mind that none of the male characters have >1k items to chose from.
