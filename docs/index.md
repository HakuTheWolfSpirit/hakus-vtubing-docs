## 3D VS 2D

2D VTubing is easy, quick and gives you a lot of creative freedom for your own designs. On the other hand 3d VTubing is complicated, painful and breaks all the time. 

If you just want to play some random desktop games a 2d VTubing model is probably best for you. You can get some good fake 3D effects with Live2D. 

But for some very specific instances investing into a 3D VTubing model might be cheaper and easier.

If you play BeatSaber or VRChat 3D is not optional. 

If you are interested in 3D VTubing skip ahead to [Basics for 3D VTubing](./3d-basics.md)
